1 Description

This is a summary of the data in the oesophagael cohort. Details:

  • 64 oesophagael adenocarcinomas: 3 without matched normal + 6 germline failed lib prep
  • Locations (for ER cohort): distal oesophagus or cardia
  • Fresh frozen
  • Treatment naive
  • Sequenced on a targeted panel
  • Treatment: en bloc surgery
